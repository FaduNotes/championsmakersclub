Meet the Founder

Mohammad Aadil

Mohammad Aadil is the founder of Champions Makers Club and a certified National Referee for Pencak Silat. With years of hands-on experience officiating and coaching at the national level, he built Champions Makers Club to pass that expertise on — training athletes not just to fight well, but to understand the sport the way it's judged and refereed at the highest level. Under his guidance, students train with discipline, technique, and the real competitive edge needed to succeed on the national stage.
